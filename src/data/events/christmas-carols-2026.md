Celebrate the season with Còisir Lunnainn's Annual Christmas Carol Service on Saturday, 5 December 2026 at 6.30pm. Join us for an evening of Gaelic lessons and traditional Christmas carols, with bilingual Gaelic-English booklets provided so everyone can take part. Whether you grew up with Gaelic or are hearing it for the first time, you'll be warmly welcomed to join in singing some of your favourite Christmas carols. After the service, stay for festive refreshments with choir members and fellow guests.
We look forward to celebrating the Christmas season with you!
